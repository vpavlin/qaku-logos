What's in the bag

A whole Q&A.
None of the server.

Run a talk, an AMA, a classroom or a town hall: anywhere people have questions and you want the best ones to float up.

Ask & upvote

Anyone in the room posts questions and upvotes the good ones. One vote per person, and you can take it back. Sort by top or newest.

Answers, accepted

The owner and admins post answers and mark the accepted one. Every answer shows who wrote it and when.

Polls

The owner and admins run polls with several options. One live vote per person, and results can stay hidden until you have voted.

Moderation & admins

The creator owns the room and can add admins. Owner and admins hide or unhide questions, open or close the room, and close or delete polls.

Sealed end to end

The room secret derives both the network topic and the encryption key. Everything is sealed on your device before it is sent.

Offline-first

Your copy of the room lives on your device. Post with no signal; it goes out when you're back, and anything you missed is backfilled.

Signed authorship

Every event is signed with your device's key, and the app checks it. Nobody can post as the host or as you.

Pseudonymous

No email, no phone number, no account. You are a key on your device. Add a display name if you want one; otherwise people see a short address.

Phone & desktop, via QR

An Android app and a Basecamp module that sync with each other. Show the room's QR on one, scan it with the other, done.

How it works

No server.
No middle-duck.

Most apps route your questions through someone's database. QAKU doesn't have one. Devices exchange sealed messages over Logos Delivery, a public peer-to-peer network, and each one folds them into the same result, whatever order they arrive in.

Notice what's missing? a server that reads your data. There isn't one.

🔐 The secret is the key

A room is a 32-byte secret. It derives the network topic and the encryption key (ChaCha20-Poly1305), so the network only moves opaque sealed bytes. Have the secret and you're in.

🧩 Event-sourced & conflict-free

Every action is a small signed event in an append-only log. Each device merges what it hears and folds it the same way (a CRDT with hybrid logical clocks), so everyone converges, whatever the order or offline gaps.

🛰️ Logos Delivery + catch-up

Messages ride Logos Delivery (Waku). When a device comes back, a catch-up exchange with the other devices in the room backfills anything it missed; the phone also pulls recent history from the network's store nodes.

✍️ Signed by you

Each event carries a secp256k1 signature; your address comes from your public key. Authorship is checkable, and owner and admin actions only count when the owner or an admin signed them.

Privacy, honestly

What's sealed

Questions, answers, votes, polls and display names are encrypted on your device with a key derived from the room secret. The network topic is derived from it too, so it doesn't reveal the room.

Who holds the key

Everyone with the room link or QR can read and post. There's no way to revoke a secret once it's shared; for a fresh audience, start a new room.

What the network sees

Relays and store nodes see sealed blobs, their topic, size and timing, not the content. Store nodes keep the sealed messages for a retention window; that's how a phone that was offline catches up.

Pseudonymous, not anonymous

Each device has one signing key, used in every room. Someone who is in two of your rooms can tell the posts come from the same device. Room members see your address and any display name you set.

📱 Android

  1. Install F-Droid.
  2. On the phone, open this link, or in F-Droid go to Settings → Repositories → + and scan the QR. The fingerprint is included, so the repository is verified.
  3. Search for QAKU and install it.

Needs an arm64 phone. Loam recommended: install Loam from the same repo and all your Logos apps share one node (plus experimental Bluetooth). Without Loam, QAKU runs its own node.

🖥️ Basecamp 0.3

  1. In Logos Basecamp, open Settings → Package Repositories and add:https://apps.vpavlin.xyz/logos-repo.json
  2. Install qaku (shown as “QAKU - Q&A”). It pulls in qaku_core (the engine and sync), which pulls in loam_core and its own dependencies (delivery_module, ble_mesh, keycard) automatically.
  3. Open QAKU and create a Q&A. It shows a join QR.

Supported on Basecamp 0.3, Linux x64, Linux ARM64 and macOS.

Using it

Five flaps and you're flying.

1
Create a Q&A
On the desktop or the phone. QAKU mints a fresh secret; that secret is the room. Add a title.
2
Scan the desktop QR
On the phone tap Scan and point it at the QR on the desktop (or projector). It joins and syncs.
3
Share with the room
Put the QR on screen or send the link. Anyone who has it can read and post, so keep it to your audience.
4
Collect & curate
Questions roll in and the best get upvoted. Answer, mark the accepted one, hide noise, run a poll.
5
Close the bag
Done? Close the room and new questions stop. Your copy stays on your devices.
Status

Where the
duck is at.

QAKU works day to day between phones and desktops. It's an early, open-source project; here's what to know.

Now: Android 0.1.78 · Basecamp qaku 0.1.28 + qaku_core 0.1.22

Limitations

  • Android: arm64 phones only.
  • Desktop: Basecamp 0.3 on Linux x64, Linux ARM64 and macOS.
  • No secret revocation: once a room's link is out, it stays readable to whoever has it.
  • One key per device across all rooms, so your posts are linkable between rooms.
  • Store nodes hold sealed messages for a retention window.
  • No public hub. An always-on hub (qaku_core running headless) is optional; run your own if you want one.

Experimental & next

  • Bluetooth (via Loam): experimental, Android only.
  • Basecamp 0.3 port: in progress.
  • Kith contacts integration: planned.
